Hindi numbers 0 to 9

Tap any card to hear it. Learn all ten in two minutes.

    Now read this

    ७५

    ७ is 7 and ५ is 5, so this is 75, said pachattar (पचहत्तर).

    Hindi numbers above 10 are said as one word, so each one is worth learning on its own.
